About

Making geospatial data meaningful, clear and accessible is what motivates me every day. I hold a Master's degree in Cartography and Geomatics (Carthagéo) from Université Paris Cité, where I developed expertise in spatial data analysis, remote sensing and the design of both static and interactive maps to support decision-making. My goal is to develop GIS solutions that transform complex spatial data into actionable information for decision-makers across a wide range of fields, including territorial planning, environmental management, infrastructure and utility networks. Throughout my academic and professional experience, I have developed skills in cartography, remote sensing, geospatial databases (PostGIS), web mapping application development (Leaflet), and programming languages including R, JavaScript and Python. Organized, detail-oriented and reliable, I work independently while also collaborating effectively within multidisciplinary teams to design solutions tailored to diverse needs. I value open-mindedness and critical thinking, enabling me to identify the most appropriate approach according to the project context and objectives. I also enjoy sharing my passion for GIS and helping users better understand and make use of geospatial information. More recently, I contributed to a project monitoring cocoa farming and deforestation in Cameroon as part of the V-Forêts programme with the Sangha Tri-National Foundation (FTNS). This experience strengthened my ability to work in an international environment, deepen my understanding of field realities, and improve the production, analysis and interpretation of geospatial data. I am currently seeking a position as a GIS Analyst, GIS Specialist or Cartographer, where I can contribute to meaningful and diverse projects, continue developing my expertise, and put geospatial data at the service of informed decision-making. I am also open to discussing opportunities with organizations looking to enhance the value of their geospatial data and develop GIS solutions tailored to their specific needs.

This portfolio showcases projects in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), web mapping and spatial analysis.
