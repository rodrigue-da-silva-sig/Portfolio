About

Making geospatial data meaningful, clear and accessible is what motivates me every day. I hold a Master's degree in Cartography and Geomatics (Carthagéo) from Université Paris Cité, where I developed expertise in spatial data analysis, remote sensing and the design of both static and interactive maps to support decision-making. My goal is to develop GIS solutions that transform complex spatial data into actionable information for decision-makers across a wide range of fields, including territorial planning, environmental management, infrastructure and utility networks. Throughout my academic and professional experience, I have developed skills in cartography, remote sensing, geospatial databases (PostGIS), web mapping application development (Leaflet), and programming languages including R, JavaScript and Python. Organized, detail-oriented and reliable, I work independently while also collaborating effectively within multidisciplinary teams to design solutions tailored to diverse needs. I value open-mindedness and critical thinking, enabling me to identify the most appropriate approach according to the project context and objectives.
